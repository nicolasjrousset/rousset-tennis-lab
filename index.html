FAQ

Common
Questions

We work with competitive junior players, tournament-level adults, and players who are serious about improving their match performance. Services are tailored to each player's level and goals.
All in-person coaching sessions are conducted at Champions Run in Omaha, Nebraska. Sessions are billed at a flat rate of $80 per hour.
We combine elite competitive experience with data-driven analysis. Every recommendation is backed by objective match data — not guesswork. Nicolas has competed and won at the highest levels of collegiate tennis, and brings that firsthand understanding to every session.
After booking, you'll receive instructions to submit your footage via a secure upload link. Most players use their phone camera — no professional recording equipment required.
Standard turnaround is 3–5 business days from when footage is received. Rush delivery options may be available — contact us for details.
A full match gives us the most complete picture, but we can work with partial footage, practice sets, or specific situations you want analyzed. The more context you provide, the more actionable the feedback.
